# Fee Policy

## 6.1 Tuition Fees

Students must pay tuition and other applicable academic fees according to the fee schedule issued for their program.

The Finance Office shall publish applicable fee amounts before each academic term.

## 6.2 Semester Fee Payment Deadline

Semester fees must normally be paid on or before 15 August for the academic term beginning in August.

Students who fail to pay fees by the deadline may be subject to late fees, registration restrictions, or other administrative action.

## 6.3 Late Payment

A late payment charge may be imposed after the published deadline.

Students facing genuine financial difficulties may submit a request for an installment arrangement.

Approval of an installment arrangement is discretionary.

## 6.4 Fee Refunds

Fee refunds shall be governed by the refund schedule published by the Finance Office.

Application fees and certain administrative charges may be non-refundable.

## 6.5 Fee Disputes

Students who believe they have been incorrectly charged may submit a written dispute to the Finance Office.

The dispute should include relevant payment records and student information.

## 6.6 Installment Payments

The Finance Office may permit eligible students to pay certain fees in installments.

The installment schedule must be approved before the first installment becomes due.

## 6.7 Late Registration Charges

Students completing registration after the prescribed registration period may be required to pay a late registration charge.

The applicable charge shall be published by the Finance Office.
