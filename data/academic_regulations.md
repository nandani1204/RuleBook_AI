# Meridian Institute of Technology
## Academic Regulations
### Academic Year 2026-27

## 1.1 Purpose

The Meridian Institute of Technology Academic Regulations establish the rules governing academic participation, registration, assessment, examinations, grading, attendance, academic progression, and appeals.

These regulations apply to all undergraduate and postgraduate students enrolled at the Institute unless a specific program regulation states otherwise.

Students are responsible for understanding the regulations applicable to their program. Ignorance of a regulation does not normally constitute grounds for exemption.

## 1.2 Scope

These regulations apply to undergraduate students, postgraduate students, diploma students, exchange students where applicable, and students participating in Institute examinations and academic activities.

Individual departments may establish additional academic procedures provided that such procedures do not contradict Institute-level regulations.

Where a departmental regulation conflicts with an Institute-level regulation, the Institute-level regulation shall normally take precedence unless the Academic Council has formally approved an exception.

## 1.3 Academic Calendar

The Institute shall publish an academic calendar before the beginning of each academic year.

The calendar normally includes:

- commencement of teaching;
- registration dates;
- add/drop period;
- assessment periods;
- examination periods;
- vacations;
- result declaration dates;
- important administrative deadlines.

Changes to the academic calendar may be approved by the Academic Council when required.

## 1.4 Registration

Students must complete academic registration within the announced registration period.

A student who fails to register within the prescribed period may be prevented from attending classes or appearing for assessments until registration is completed.

Late registration may be permitted by the Academic Office in exceptional circumstances. A late registration fee may apply.

Students must verify their registered courses after registration. Errors should normally be reported to the department within seven working days.

## 1.5 Course Selection

Students must select courses according to the requirements of their academic program.

Prerequisite courses must normally be completed before enrolling in courses that depend on them.

The department may restrict registration where a student has not satisfied prerequisite requirements.

## 1.6 Academic Advising

Students may seek guidance from their assigned academic advisor regarding course selection, academic progression, and other academic matters.

Academic advisors may recommend courses but students remain responsible for ensuring that their registration satisfies program requirements.

## 1.7 Academic Progression

Students must satisfy the minimum academic requirements established by their program to progress to the next academic year.

A student who fails to meet progression requirements may be required to repeat courses or complete additional academic requirements.

## 1.8 Academic Integrity

Students are expected to maintain academic honesty in assignments, examinations, projects, laboratory work, and other academic activities.

Plagiarism, fabrication, unauthorized collaboration, impersonation, cheating, and submission of work prepared entirely by another person may constitute academic misconduct.

## 1.9 Plagiarism

Academic work submitted by a student must appropriately acknowledge sources.

The Institute may use plagiarism-detection software or other methods to evaluate submitted work.

The consequences of plagiarism shall depend on the seriousness and circumstances of the case.

## 1.10 Academic Records

The Institute maintains academic records including course registrations, grades, attendance records, examination results, and other information required for academic administration.

Students may request correction of inaccurate academic information through the appropriate administrative procedure.

## 1.11 Academic Appeals

Students may appeal an academic decision where they believe relevant circumstances or evidence were not properly considered.

An appeal should identify the decision being challenged, the reason for the appeal, supporting evidence, and the remedy requested.

## 1.12 Interpretation of Academic Regulations

Where the meaning of an academic provision is unclear, the Academic Office may provide an administrative interpretation.

Where two provisions appear to conflict, the matter may be referred to the Academic Committee.

The Academic Committee may recommend an interpretation to the Academic Council.

## 1.13 Amendments

The Institute may amend academic regulations through the approved policy-making process.

Approved amendments shall normally be communicated through official Institute channels.

## 1.14 Effective Date

These regulations are effective from 1 August 2026 and remain applicable until amended or replaced.
