# Scholarship Policy

## 7.1 Scholarship Categories

The Institute may provide scholarships based on academic performance, financial circumstances, sports achievement, cultural achievement, innovation, entrepreneurship, or other criteria approved by the Institute.

Scholarship categories and eligibility requirements shall be published annually.

## 7.2 Academic Merit Scholarship

Students receiving an academic merit scholarship must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.5 to retain the scholarship for the following academic year.

Students falling below the specified CGPA may lose eligibility unless an approved exception applies.

## 7.3 Scholarship Application

Students must submit scholarship applications within the published application period.

Applications submitted after the deadline may not be considered.

Required documents may include academic records, income documentation, achievement certificates, or other relevant evidence.

## 7.4 Scholarship Review

Scholarship awards may be reviewed at the end of each academic year.

The Scholarship Committee may suspend or terminate an award when eligibility conditions are no longer satisfied.

## 7.5 Scholarship Continuation

Students who maintain a CGPA of 7.0 or above may continue receiving an academic scholarship, provided that they satisfy the other scholarship conditions.

## 7.6 Financial Need Scholarship

Students applying under a financial-need category may be required to provide income or financial documentation.

The Scholarship Committee may request additional information when necessary.

## 7.7 Scholarship Misrepresentation

Providing false information or fraudulent documentation in a scholarship application may result in cancellation of the scholarship and disciplinary action.

## 7.8 Scholarship Appeal

A student whose scholarship is rejected, suspended, or terminated may submit an appeal to the Scholarship Committee.

The appeal should include the reasons for requesting reconsideration and relevant supporting evidence.
