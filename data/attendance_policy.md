# Attendance Policy

## 2.1 General Attendance Requirement

Students are expected to attend all scheduled lectures, tutorials, laboratory sessions, practical classes, seminars, and other compulsory academic activities.

Attendance shall be recorded by the concerned faculty member or through an approved digital attendance system.

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

Attendance shall be calculated using the number of sessions attended divided by the number of sessions conducted, excluding sessions officially cancelled by the Institute.

## 2.2 Attendance Monitoring

Faculty members shall periodically make attendance records available to students.

Students are responsible for checking their attendance and reporting apparent errors.

A student who believes that an attendance record is incorrect should raise the issue with the concerned faculty member before the announced attendance correction deadline.

## 2.3 Attendance Shortage

A student whose attendance falls below 75% shall normally be declared ineligible to appear for the corresponding end-semester examination.

The Academic Office may publish a list of students with attendance shortages before examinations.

Students may submit an appeal when they believe their shortage resulted from circumstances recognized under Institute regulations.

## 2.4 Practical Course Attendance

For courses containing laboratory or practical components, attendance may be maintained separately.

Students may be required to complete missed practical sessions before receiving examination eligibility.

## 2.5 Institute Representation

Students officially representing the Institute in recognized competitions, conferences, sports events, cultural activities, or academic events may request attendance consideration.

Such consideration normally requires prior authorization from the appropriate Institute authority.

## 2.6 Medical Absence

Students unable to attend academic activities because of illness may submit appropriate medical documentation.

Medical documentation should normally identify the period of illness and the relevant medical practitioner.

## 2.7 Medical Attendance Relaxation

A student with an approved medical exemption may be permitted to appear for an examination when attendance is not below 60%, subject to approval by the Academic Office.

Submission of a medical certificate does not automatically guarantee an exemption.

## 2.8 Medical Exemption Procedure

Applications for medical attendance consideration should normally be submitted within seven working days after the student's return to academic activities.

The Head of Department may review the application before forwarding it to the Academic Office.

The Academic Office may approve, reject, or request additional documentation.

## 2.9 Emergency Circumstances

In cases involving hospitalization or other serious medical circumstances, the Institute may consider applications submitted after the normal deadline.

The decision shall be based on available documentation and the circumstances of the case.
