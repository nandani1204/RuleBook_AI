# Examination Regulations

## 4.1 Examination Eligibility

Only students who satisfy registration, attendance, and other applicable academic requirements may appear for an end-semester examination.

The Examination Office shall publish an eligibility list before examinations begin.

Students should verify their eligibility before the examination period.

## 4.2 Examination Schedule

The examination timetable shall be published through official Institute communication channels.

Students should arrive at the examination venue at least fifteen minutes before the scheduled start time unless otherwise instructed.

## 4.3 Examination Identification

Students must carry their valid Institute identification card or another identity document authorized by the Examination Office.

A student unable to produce acceptable identification may be temporarily denied entry until identity can be verified.

## 4.4 Examination Conduct

Students must follow instructions issued by the invigilator.

Unless specifically authorized, students may not use mobile phones, smart watches, unauthorized electronic devices, written notes, or unauthorized reference material during an examination.

Communication with other candidates during an examination is prohibited unless permitted by the invigilator.

## 4.5 Late Arrival

Students arriving after the examination has started may be permitted to enter only with authorization from the examination supervisor.

A student arriving significantly late may be refused entry where examination security requires such action.

## 4.6 Absence From Examination

A student who does not attend an examination shall normally receive a result corresponding to non-attendance unless another academic procedure applies.

Medical absence may be considered under the medical provisions of the Attendance Policy.

Other circumstances may be considered only where specifically recognized by the Examination Office or Academic Council.

## 4.7 Examination Materials

Students must use only materials authorized for the particular examination.

Unauthorized material may be confiscated and may result in an academic misconduct investigation.

## 4.8 Re-examination

Students failing a course may be eligible for a supplementary or repeat examination subject to program rules and the academic calendar.

Eligibility for a re-examination does not guarantee a particular grade.

## 4.9 Examination Results

Examination results shall be published through the official academic system.

Students should report apparent errors according to the result-review procedure.
