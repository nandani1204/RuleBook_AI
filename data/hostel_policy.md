# Hostel Regulations

## 8.1 Hostel Allocation

Hostel rooms shall be allocated according to availability, program requirements, student category, and criteria determined by Hostel Administration.

Room allocation does not create permanent entitlement to a particular room.

## 8.2 Hostel Entry and Exit

Residents must comply with hostel entry and exit procedures.

The Hostel Administration may maintain movement records for safety and security purposes.

## 8.3 Visitors

Visitors may enter hostel premises only during designated visiting hours and must comply with registration requirements.

Residents are responsible for the conduct of their visitors.

Overnight visitors are prohibited unless written permission has been granted by Hostel Administration.

## 8.4 Room Changes

Students may request a room change by submitting an application to the Hostel Office.

Room changes are subject to availability and approval.

## 8.5 Hostel Conduct

Residents must maintain reasonable cleanliness and avoid behavior that disturbs other residents.

Unauthorized electrical equipment, property damage, disruptive behavior, and unauthorized guests are prohibited.

## 8.6 Quiet Hours

Residents should observe designated quiet hours.

Activities that create unreasonable noise during quiet hours may result in a warning or disciplinary action.

## 8.7 Hostel Maintenance

Students should report maintenance problems to the Hostel Office.

Students may be held responsible for intentional damage to hostel property.
